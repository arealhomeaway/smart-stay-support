## A. Scoring rules (all plans)

- Each goal pays its SHARE of the dollar amount for the level reached on that goal (Goal met,
  Strong, Exceeds). Below goal pays $0 for that goal. The goal payments are added.
- Payout is NOT a percent multiplier. It is a dollar amount per level per plan. Strong sits
  halfway between Goal met and Exceeds.
- Maximum: never more than the Exceeds amount.
- Not counted: a goal with nothing to measure in the period is skipped and its share is moved
  proportionally to the person's other goals. If fewer than 3 goals can be measured, the person
  gets the full Goal met amount for the period.
- System problem: if a system or tracking error affects a goal, it counts at Goal met or the
  actual level, whichever is higher. The reviewer flags it with a reason and the COO approves.
- Excused records (approved leave, work outside the assigned shift, outages) are left out only with
  a reason AND the COO's approval. A reviewer cannot exclude a fact alone.
- Deadlines never move: a new date does not erase the original.
- Off shift means off: the on duty person owns it.
- Reviewing chain: the Supervisor's scorecard is reviewed by the Director; the Director's by the
  COO; nobody approves their own; the COO settles questions about the Supervisor; if the COO and the
  Director disagree, the CEO decides.
- A person has 3 business days after receiving their scorecard to raise a question (a Raise a
  question button that closes after 3 business days).
- Pays on the last payday of the month after the period (November pays the last payday in
  December; the Director's pilot pays the last payday in January 2027). Show a Pays on date.
- Imported Notion tickets, test records and tracking before a person's counting start never count.

   approves; score = fix plans finished by their original deadline; reviewer entered until fix plan
   records exist.
3. Team reliability: PC on time follow through and team duties on time combined, automatic.
4. Finance packets every Friday: Emily retired the weekly Friday finance packet. Seed it as written
   but INACTIVE (Not counted, its share moves) until Emily decides what replaces it.
5. Quarterly priorities: the COO sets 100 points of priorities with the Director before each
   period. Points count once a priority is accepted, used for 30 days and shows its agreed result.
   A priorities editor the COO can add to by hand: name, points, status (Proposed, Accepted, In use
   (30 days), Result agreed), the result note; the total must reach 100; points counted = points of
   priorities that are accepted, used 30 days and have an agreed result. Anything meant to count in
   the pilot must be accepted by about Dec 1.
6. Vendor coverage: 6 markets, each with a primary and a backup Approved or Gold vendor in HVAC,
   plumbing, handyman and electrical: 48 slots; score = slots filled; reviewer entered until a
   coverage table exists.

Settings page: the COO, the CEO and Admin can add a goal, edit its name, share, three thresholds
with their units and the measure source, and mark it Active or Inactive, with a log of every change
and the note that changes to goals, targets or amounts need 30 days' written notice and apply only
going forward.

## G. Corrections to Build 14 (records check, coaching, weekly report)

1. The weekly draw is 5 records PER ACTIVE PC (about 20 a month), a setting with default 5 per PC.
2. Add the Director's double check: each week the Director double checks at least 5 of the
   Supervisor's record checks (5 of the week's reviews chosen at random, each with Agree or Disagree
   and a note); a record changed after the Director's check goes with the Director's call; the
   number checked is a setting, default 5.
3. Coaching is one session per PC per month (required count = number of active PCs); PCs with fewer
   than 10 shifts get onboarding coaching that counts.
4. The weekly scorecard covers work through Wednesday.
