# Email template drafts: ARHA Onboarding - Standard

Draft for Kati to review. Nothing in the app or the data was changed, and no email was sent.

Each template below is copied word for word from the step that contains it. Placeholders in curly braces (for example {owner first name}) are kept exactly as written; they would become merge fields later. Steps that only say to email someone, with no written message, are listed at the end.

## 1. Introduction email and schedule the Owner readiness call

- From step: Send the introduction email and schedule the Owner readiness call (Intake)
- Audience: Homeowner
- Subject: Welcome to A Real Home Away! Let's Get Your Readiness Call Scheduled

Body:

```text
Hi {owner first names},
It's so nice to meet you, and congratulations on going under contract on {property address}! We're excited to partner with you on this home.
I'm {your first name}, {your title} at A Real Home Away, and I'll be guiding you through the process of getting your home set up, furnished, and guest ready. Thank you, {DFY contact}, for the introduction!
The first step is an Owner readiness call with Emily Jimenez, our COO. On this call, Emily will walk you through how we work together, what to expect from onboarding, and how you'll be involved along the way. With your closing date set for {closing date}, we want to get started as soon as possible so you're set up for success from day one.
Could you share a few days and times that work for you over the next week? Please include your time zone, and we'll get something on the calendar.
Looking forward to working with you!
Warmly,
{your name}
{your title}
A Real Home Away
```
