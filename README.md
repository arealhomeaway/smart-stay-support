# Home Harmony Hub

I am the COO at A Real Home Away. We are a STR & MTR property managment company. have an idea for an app for my internal team to help them with decision making with maintenance and active guests to solve issues quickly and thouroughly. One example of a way that we need to refine our process is: yesterday, an active guest messaged us and said there was a leak under the sink. We sent a handyman to fix it and when he left, the guest reported it wasn't all the way fixed. Meanwhile, there was an alarm that kept going off and the guest couldn't figure out where it was from. The handyman went back, removed the fire alarm and left. They left a hole in the wall and they left the guest at risk of not having a fire alarm. Meanwhile, throughout the night, the guest reported they still heard the alarm. They ended up finding where it was coming from and it was the carbon monoxide alarm. They called the fire department and the fire department found that it was coming from the water heater and they turned the water heater off. I think all of this could have been fixed, if the property coordinator had a way of learning how to communicate better with the handyman. The handyman should have been instructed to check all fire alarms, security systems and carbon monoxide alarms in the home. He should have also been instructed that if any alarms need to be replaced to remove them and then replace them. Not to simply remove them and leave a hole in the wall. All of this seems common sense. But, we are finding that it is not. Can you build an app for me that helps my team to be able to solve problems like this and other situations that they  might run  into?
